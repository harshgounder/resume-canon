# 10. Variants

There is no universal resume. There are genres, and each genre has a market.

> Evidence: wave 05 (India campus, 6,945 words, primary placement cell
> policies), wave 06 (2026 vendor and field state), wave 01 lane D (authorities).

## The two axes

Every resume decision depends on two things:

1. **Who reads it first.** A parser, a recruiter, a hiring manager, a placement
   cell, a committee, or a portfolio link.
2. **What they are allowed to care about.** Some markets legally cannot see your
   photo. Some require it. Some require proof of every claim.

Get those wrong and the craft does not matter.

The rule behind the table: a photo is a signal. In the US it signals
unfamiliarity with hiring norms and creates bias exposure. In Japan it signals
that you read the instructions. Neither is about the photo's quality.

## India: a gated document, not a marketing one

This is the largest structural difference in the corpus, and US guides do not
describe it because it does not exist there.

**The placement cell is a gatekeeper with verification and penalties.**

- IIT Madras requires proof for every claim except institute positions of
  responsibility, runs two verification rounds, blocks applications until the
  resume is approved, and treats false information as grounds for expulsion from
  placement (V1, placement policy).
- IIT Bombay classifies resume falsification as a "Dark Violation" with penalty
  points (V1).
- NIT Kurukshetra bars a student from the next five consecutive placement
  opportunities for misrepresentation (V1).
- IIT Hyderabad generates the CV on the portal in a standard format, verifies a
  master CV in three rounds, and does not allow alteration after submission (V1).
- IIT Delhi builds the CV on the OCS portal and requires a photo there (V1).
- NIT Trichy instructs students not to change font, size, or template (V1).

**The number of resumes is capped by the cell, not chosen by you.** IIT Madras
allows five fixed resumes plus one master containing every point and its proof
(V1). The master is the source of truth; the five are cuts of it.

**The photo rule is channel specific, and the primary sources contradict each
other.**

- IIT Delhi requires a photo on the portal (V1).
- Naukri tells candidates to remove the photo from the document and attach it to
  the profile instead (V1).
- TCS Atlas says not to include a photo or hyperlinks in the CV content (V1).

So any single national rule is wrong. The correct behaviour is: follow the
channel you are using, and when the channel is silent, omit the photo.

**TCS NQT inverts the US order.** The test comes first and the application form
can be filled after results are declared. The CV upload sits in the Smart Card
details tab and is for the company ID card at joining, not for shortlisting
(V1). Constraints are print constraints: 500KB maximum, no photo, no hyperlinks.
Eligibility: 60 percent across class 10, 12, diploma if applicable, graduation
and post graduation, no pending backlogs, no extended education, gap of at most
24 months, age 18 to 28 (V1, TCS).

**Cutoffs are arithmetic applied before content.** VIT states that 59.9 percent
is not 60 and does not round CGPA (V1). IIT Madras uses a backlog count of three
or fewer plus a credit floor rather than a CGPA floor (V1). No resume wording
changes any of this. Check eligibility first.

**One Student, One Job.** NIT Warangal, NIT Karnataka, VIT and NIT Trichy end a
student's season at the first accepted offer (V1). That has no US equivalent and
changes strategy: the first acceptable offer is the last one you get.

**CodeVita is a contest, not an application.** No CV upload appears in its public
registration flow. The CV an interviewer holds comes from the NextStep form or
from the candidate (V1, public pages; the logged-in area could not be checked).

### What to change when applying from India to abroad

- Remove photo, date of birth, father's name, marital status, address beyond
  city.
- Switch to Letter for US and Canada.
- Drop the declaration line and the "I hereby declare" block.
- Rewrite formal register into direct verbs.
- Keep the CGPA with its scale, drop the percentage if the market does not use
  it.
- Keep contest ranks with a denominator; they travel well.

## Special cases

**Employment gap.** No ATS detects gaps. Knockout questions and recruiter filters
do the work. Date the gap honestly, and if it was caregiving, study, health, or a
layoff, one line in the summary handles it. Never switch to a functional resume:
it removes the dated records the filters read.

**Career switch.** Summary first, then projects that prove the new capability,
then experience reframed toward transferable outcomes. The reader needs a reason
to believe the switch is real, and a course certificate is not a reason.

**Returning after a break.** Same as a gap, plus one line of current evidence:
a recent project, a course with an output, freelance work.

**Overqualified.** The reader's first question is "why this job". Answer it in the
summary, and cut the roles that make you look expensive.

**International student.** Two documents: one for the local market with
authorisation status stated plainly, one for home.

**Founder or self-employed.** Title yourself by function ("Founder, 3 person
team") and give the outcome, not the org chart. A founder title with no scale
reads as unverifiable.
